Rabbit Hole terms

Last updated 1 October 2026

These are the rules for using Rabbit Hole, in plain words. By signing in or using the site, you agree to them.

What Rabbit Hole is

Rabbit Hole is a free research board. It searches public archives and websites for you, and AI suggests how the things it finds might connect.

AI can be wrong

The strings, summaries, timelines and answers are made by AI from what the sources say. They can be incomplete, out of date or simply wrong, especially about real people. Treat them as leads to check, not as facts. Always read the original source before you rely on something or repeat it. Don't use Rabbit Hole for legal, medical, financial or safety decisions.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Rabbit Hole.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Each account has a daily allowance of digs and partner messages, so the free AI services stay available for everyone.

Your boards and what you share

Your notes and boards are yours. When you share a board by link, anyone with the link can see it (but not your chat with the partner), and they can make their own copy. You are responsible for what you share. Don't use Rabbit Hole to:

  • spread claims about a real person that you know are false, or present an AI guess as proven fact;
  • harass, threaten or target anyone, or post someone's private information (home address, phone number, and so on);
  • share content that is illegal, hateful, or sexual content involving minors;
  • spam, overload the service, or get around the daily allowance (for example with many accounts or automated tools).

Anyone can report a shared board with the Report button on it. We may turn off a share link, remove a board's sharing, or close an account that breaks these rules.

Other people's work

Cards point to material from archives, news sites, museums and other sources. That material belongs to its owners. Rabbit Hole shows short excerpts and links to the original; follow each source's own terms if you reuse it.

The service itself

Rabbit Hole is provided as it is, without any guarantee. It relies on free outside services, so features can be slow, change, or stop working, and the site may change or close. Keep a copy of anything important (Settings → Save & share exports a board). As far as the law allows, Rabbit Hole isn't liable for losses that come from using it or relying on what it shows.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes too. Using the site after a change means you accept the new terms.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/Juhainah/Rabbit-Hole. See also the privacy page.
