Rabbit Hole privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026 · see also the terms

Rabbit Hole is a free research board. This page explains, in plain words, what it knows about you and where your information goes.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub, or an email and password. Sign-in is handled by Supabase. Rabbit Hole receives your email address, your name, and your profile picture if your account has one. They are used only to let you in and to keep the free research tools from being overused. Your password is never seen by Rabbit Hole; Supabase stores it securely.

Your boards

Your boards, cards, notes and chats with the partner are saved in your browser and, while you're signed in, in your account (stored with Supabase), so they're still there on another device or after clearing your browser. Only you can see them. Deleting a board removes its contents from your account too.

If you share a board, anyone with its link can see that board's cards and strings, and copy it. Your chat with the partner is never included. You can turn a link off at any time with Share → Stop sharing.

What happens when you research

When you dig into a topic or ask the research partner a question, your search words, and the cards and notes needed to answer, are sent to Rabbit Hole's server. The server passes them to public archives and search services (such as Wikipedia and the Internet Archive) and to third-party AI services to find and summarise sources. Those services receive the text of the request, not your name or email. Don't put private information in searches or notes you ask the partner about.

Counting and error reports

  • For each account, Rabbit Hole counts how many digs and partner messages it uses each day, and the days it visits. This keeps the daily allowance fair and shows how many people use the site. It's a count, not a record of what you searched.
  • If something breaks while you use the site, the error message, where in the app it happened, the page address and your browser type are saved with your account id, so the problem can be fixed.
  • If you report a shared board, the reason and anything you write in the report are saved.

What Rabbit Hole doesn't do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No tracking of what you read or search for. Basic server logs are kept briefly by the hosting service to keep the site running.

Deleting your account

You can delete any board yourself at any time. To have your whole account deleted (sign-in, saved boards, counts and error reports), ask through the contact link below.
